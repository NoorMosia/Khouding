# p10, Stats card (Family C, model and format)

**Integrates:** types + naming + compute `+` (1.3) + tab-aligned output (1.2).

## Statement (student-facing)
A player card for `Mara`, with `9` goals and `6` assists. Points is goals plus assists. Declare well-named
variables, compute points, and print three tab-separated label lines:

```
Player:	Mara
Goals:	9
Points:	15
```

Use `Write` for the label (ending with `\t`) and `WriteLine` for the value.

## Why it belongs in the boss
Model-and-format with a computed `+` total and tab alignment. A value-varied variant.
