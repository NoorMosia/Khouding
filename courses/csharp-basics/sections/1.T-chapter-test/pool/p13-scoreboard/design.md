# p13, Scoreboard (Family C, model and format)

**Integrates:** types + naming + compute `+` (1.3) + output with a blank line and tabs (1.2).

## Statement (student-facing)
A scoreboard: `Lions` 2, `Bears` 3. Total goals is the sum. Print the heading, a blank line, then each team
and score separated by a tab, then a total line:

```
SCOREBOARD

Lions	2
Bears	3
Total goals:	5
```

## Why it belongs in the boss
Combines a blank line, tab alignment, two string/int pairs, and a computed total. The fullest
model-and-format layout in the pool. A value-varied variant.
