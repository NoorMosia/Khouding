# Problem 05, Declare with good names (language-independent design)

**Sub-skill:** produce good camelCase names from scratch (convention).
**Difficulty:** 1

## Statement (student-facing)
Read a first name on the first line and an age on the second line. Store each in a well-named camelCase
variable, then print the name, then the age, each on its own line.

## I/O contract
- **Input:** two lines: a first name, then an age.
- **Output:** two lines: the name, then the age.

## Why it resists a trivial answer
Varied inputs mean only a program that reads both values and prints them passes. The positive counterpart
to p04: here you choose good names (`firstName`, `age`) from the start. The quiz carries the judgment of
which names follow convention.
