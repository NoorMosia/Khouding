# Pool p01, Call a helper once (family A, define & call)

**Integrates:** 6.1.
**Difficulty:** 1

## Statement (student-facing)
Define a `static void` method `Hello` that prints exactly `hello`. Call it once from `Main`.

## I/O contract
- **Input:** none.
- **Output:** `hello` on one line.

## Why it resists a trivial answer
The method must be defined and called. Later pool problems build on this with parameters and returns.
